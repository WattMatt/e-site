In the matter regarding the

Municipal Tariff Application for the 2026/27 Financial Year

By

City Power (Johannesburg) (Pty) Ltd
___________________________________________________________________

                                     THE DECISION



1. At its meeting held on 11 May 2026, the Regulator Executive Committee (REC)
   considered City Power’s tariff application. Based on the facts and evidence
   presented, the REC approved the tariff increases for the 2026/27 financial year, as
   set out in the tariff analysis section of this document.

2. Based on the available information and the analysis performed, the REC decided:

     a) to approve the tariffs that are based on the cost of supply (COS) study, as
        recommended in the tariff analysis section, for implementation by the licensee
        with effect from 1 July 2026;
     b) to note the COS study submitted by the licensee;
     c) that the licensee must submit a report accounting for all the costs identified in
        its COS study to enable the National Energy Regulator of South Africa to
        perform its independent verification or audit process by 31 January 2027; and
     d) that the licensee must submit a quarterly smart efficiency plan with improvement
        measures identified for reducing high energy losses and submit it by
        30 September 2026.
