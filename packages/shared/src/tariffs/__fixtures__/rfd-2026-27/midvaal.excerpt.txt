In the matter regarding the

Municipal Tariff Application for the 2026/27 Financial Year

By

Midvaal Local Municipality
___________________________________________________________________

                                      THE DECISION



1. At its meeting held on 30 April 2026, the Regulator Executive Committee (REC)
   considered the Midvaal Local Municipality’s tariff application. Based on the facts
   and evidence presented, the REC approved the tariff increases for the 2026/27
   financial year, as set out in the tariff analysis section of this document.

2. Based on the available information and the analysis performed, the REC decided:

     2.1.   to approve the tariffs that are based on the cost drivers, as recommended in
            the tariff analysis section, for implementation by the municipality with effect
            from 1 July 2026;
     2.2.   to note the cost drivers submitted by the municipality;
     2.3.   that the municipality must submit a report accounting for all the costs noted
            in its cost drivers to enable the National Energy Regulator of South Africa to
            perform its independent verification or audit process by 31 October 2026;
            and
     2.4.   that the municipality must submit an audited financial report that reflects the
            impact of its cost driver-based tariffs on customers, the licensee’s
            sustainability and local economy by 31 October 2026.
