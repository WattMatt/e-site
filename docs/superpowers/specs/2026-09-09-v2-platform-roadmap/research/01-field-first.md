# Field-first / site-capture products — competitive research for E-Site v2

Researched 2026-09-09. Products: Fieldwire (Hilti), PlanRadar, Dalux Field, Raken, CompanyCam, Novade. Prices are list prices as published on the vendors' own pages on that date unless flagged as aggregator estimates.

---

## 1. Fieldwire (Hilti)

**JTBD / daily user.** "Task-on-plan" coordination for foremen, superintendents and project engineers: every task is a pin on a drawing sheet with assignee, due date, priority, category, checklist and photos. 4,000,000+ projects claimed ([fieldwire.com/blog/create-punch-list-report](https://www.fieldwire.com/blog/create-punch-list-report/)). Reviewers cite punch lists, RFIs and progress tracking as the core uses ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/fieldwire-by-hilti/reviews)).

**Daily loop.** Open the sheet → see your pins → work → update status (custom statuses on Pro+) → photos with metadata stamps. Tasks have *followers*; a task update fires an email to every follower except the author **5 minutes after** the update, batched so it "includes all updates since it was first triggered" and **capped at one email per hour** per task ([help.fieldwire.com Task notifications](https://help.fieldwire.com/hc/en-us/articles/206204800-Task-notifications-)). Per-user preference is *instantly / daily / never*; the daily summary lands at **05:00 in the project's timezone**, and support can force an org-wide daily summary that overrides individual "never" settings ([Introduction to Notifications](https://help.fieldwire.com/hc/en-us/articles/360005029011-Introduction-to-Notifications)).

**Praised.** Ease of use / fast crew onboarding; linking tasks to drawings; markup tools and sheet hyperlinks; reports "in one click" ([G2 pros/cons](https://www.g2.com/products/fieldwire-by-hilti/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons), [Capterra 4.6, 96 reviews](https://capterra.com/p/142801/Fieldwire/reviews/)).
**Top complaints.** (1) No way to sort plan pages — "hours of renaming pages" on large projects; (2) thin integrations with job-management/ERP systems → admin double entry; (3) clunky spots such as the signature flow ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/fieldwire-by-hilti/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons)); plus slow plan uploads in Oct-2025 app-store reports ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/780165517/fieldwire-construction-manager/problems)).

**Reporting.** Task reports = *PDF Summary* (bullet list for simple punch lists) or *Detailed PDF* (photos, annotations, messages, plan crops), filterable by priority/category/hashtag/assignee/location so a GC can send each subcontractor only its own items, with the company logo, to people "even if they're not a Fieldwire user"; recurring reports auto-send daily/weekly/monthly; full CSV dump ([Task Reports KB](https://help.fieldwire.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000574203-Introduction-to-Task-Reports-in-Fieldwire), [punch list blog](https://www.fieldwire.com/blog/create-punch-list-report/)). Reports & exports are **not** on the free tier.

**Mobile.** Native iOS/Android/Windows; you *enable* projects for offline; "Smart Sync" limits full sync to Wi-Fi by default without blocking offline use ([Fieldwire on Mobile](https://www.fieldwire.com/support/fieldwire-on-mobile/)). Photo metadata stamps and 360° photos are paid features.

**Pricing.** Basic **$0** (5 users, 3 projects, 100 sheets: plan viewing, tasks, files, photos, specs) · Pro **$39** · Business **$59** · Business Plus **$89** per user/month annual (monthly $54/$74/$104). Custom fields, custom forms, BIM viewer, 360° = Business; RFIs, submittals, change orders, budget = Business Plus. ZAR is a selectable currency ([fieldwire.com/pricing](https://www.fieldwire.com/pricing/)).

**External-party adoption.** The free Basic tier is the sub's on-ramp; task followers get email without opening the app; reports are the way non-users participate. No true guest/email-only role beyond that.

## 2. PlanRadar

**JTBD / daily user.** Ticket-on-plan defect/snag management for consultants, site managers and FM; three built-in role classes — *in-house users* create/edit/delete tickets, *subcontractors* can only update tickets assigned to them, *watchers* view only ([Tickets KB](https://help.planradar.com/hc/en-gb/articles/13977954738845-Tickets)).

**Daily loop.** Assigned tickets + due-date reminders + a choice of one digest a day at a user-chosen time or an immediate mail per update. Scope selector: *any event in all projects* / *only tickets where I'm assignee, receiver or in a user field* / *me or my groups* / *none*. Separate opt-in "daily Documents update email" listing files uploaded/updated across projects. Due-date reminders are sent "a few days before" at a configured day and time ([Configure E-Mail Notifications](https://help.planradar.com/hc/en-gb/articles/15191969506461-Configure-E-Mail-Notifications)). An account-level switch decides whether adding media/comments counts as a ticket "update" (and hence notifies) ([Account Settings](https://help.planradar.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16273731165597-Account-Settings)). Tickets can be linked to printed **QR codes**; scanning opens the ticket ([Tickets KB](https://www.planradar.com/knowledge-base/tickets/)).

**Praised.** Organisation and defect tracking; "solid platform for a consultancy business"; keeps everyone on the same page ([Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/156698/PlanRadar/reviews/), aggregate 4.3 ([planradar.com reviews page](https://www.planradar.com/us/planradar-reviews/))).
**Top complaints.** (1) Limited annotation customisation and detailed notes on plans; (2) overwhelming learning curve at start; (3) a $2,000+ auto-renewal cancellation trap; also camera selection limited to front/rear ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/156698/PlanRadar/reviews/), [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/planradar/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons)).

**Reporting.** Ticket reports for any filtered selection, PDF or Excel, up to 2,000 tickets per report. *Basic* templates (all plans) pick which ticket attributes to include; the *Template Builder* (Pro/Enterprise only) gives full layout control, plus Excel export templates ([Ticket Reports](https://help.planradar.com/hc/en-gb/articles/13791785308317-Ticket-Reports), [Advanced templates](https://help.planradar.com/hc/en-gb/articles/13258004011549-Create-Advanced-Ticket-Report-Templates)). Daily reports are just another form template — the vendor's own tutorial builds one template for the construction manager (bottlenecks) and a different one for the building owner (finished tasks + tomorrow's locations) ([daily reporting app](https://www.planradar.com/daily-reporting-app/)).

**Mobile.** Native iOS, Android **and Windows**; tickets/voice memos created offline sync on reconnect; user picks "mobile available projects" in the web app; recently opened files are cached, unavailable ones greyed out ([System Requirements](https://help.planradar.com/hc/en-gb/articles/12274938036125-System-Requirements), [pricing FAQ](https://www.planradar.com/pricing/)).

**Pricing.** Per *in-house* user: Basic **$32/month, max 1 user, 10 digital plans**; Starter **$107/user/month (≤10 users, 30 plans/user)**; Pro and Enterprise higher (aggregators quote ~$239 Pro). **Unlimited subcontractors and watchers free on every package**, unlimited projects and exports ([planradar.com/pricing](https://www.planradar.com/pricing/)). Plans (sheets) are the metered unit, not projects.

**External-party adoption.** This is the model: pay for the core team, invite unlimited subs (update-only) and watchers (read-only) free. Subs receive email on assignment and can reply from the app or web.

## 3. Dalux Field

**JTBD / daily user.** BIM-first site QA/QC, snagging, H&S and handover for main contractors and consultants, heavy in Scandinavia/UK. Site engineers pin tasks to drawings or BIM objects; office tracks progress live ([dalux.com/dalux-field](https://www.dalux.com/dalux-field/)).

**Daily loop.** Tasks with "a deadline and a responsible person for every task"; users grouped into *teams that only see tasks relevant to them* to avoid supply-chain information overload ([Field Basic page](https://www.dalux.com/products/dalux-field-basic/)). **Location QR codes**: a printed code on a room/asset opens that location with all its issues, checklists and inspections; codes can also open a BIM object ([QR codes for Locations](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/10238784116252-QR-codes-for-Locations)). **Public safety-observation QR** on the site fence lets *anyone, non-members included*, submit an observation. Push notifications per task type can be toggled on mobile or in the profile; a **daily summary email** exists and now includes public safety observations ([release notes 2026.2](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/25721458580508-Build-release-notes-2026-2)).

**Praised.** Intuitive UI, fast handling of large BIM models, "virtually foolproof" for site staff; G2 4.6 (176 reviews), Capterra 4.5 ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/154695/Dalux-Field/reviews/), [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/dalux/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons)).
**Top complaints.** (1) Data loss "when out of range … reports sometimes being lost"; (2) editing/authoring is thin — great viewer, weak model manipulation, no assembly levels; (3) Revit-only model ingestion, large-model performance ([SelectHub](https://www.selecthub.com/p/building-information-modeling-software/dalux/), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/154695/Dalux-Field/reviews/)).

**Reporting.** Checklists/forms with hold points and metadata; self-inspections with photos pinned to drawing or BIM object; PDF from list views; safety observations printable as PDF ([QC solution page](https://www.dalux.com/solutions/quality-control-and-assurance/)).

**Mobile.** Native iOS/Android (one "Dalux" app), works offline "e.g. in a basement"; desktop *Dalux Sync* for offline files; changelog still shipping offline improvements in 2026 ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=air.dk.dalux.daluxqa), [Dalux Sync](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/360015301274-Dalux-Sync)).

**Pricing.** **Field Basic is free with unlimited users per project** and unlimited tasks, drawings and 3D models; Field (full) and BIM Viewer are quote-based enterprise licences ([Field Basic](https://www.dalux.com/products/dalux-field-basic/), [SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/construction/dalux-field)).

**External-party adoption.** Freemium with no seat cap is the wedge — the whole supply chain joins at zero cost; the team-scoped visibility and QR-on-fence public submissions extend participation to people with no account at all.

## 4. Raken

**JTBD / daily user.** The daily report, done by the superintendent/foreman on a phone at end of shift: "walk the site, narrate the day into your phone, snap a few photos, and the app ships a branded PDF" ([Contractor ToolStack review](https://contractortoolstack.com/software/raken/)). Plus time cards, toolbox talks, observations, checklists, production tracking.

**Daily loop.** Push **reminder at a time of your choosing** to complete the daily ([Vimeo tutorial](https://vimeo.com/146017716)); report structure = work logs, manpower, survey questions, photos, auto weather; report auto-shares field→office on completion ([features/daily-reports](https://www.rakenapp.com/features/daily-reports)). **Super Daily** merges the GC's report with every collaborator (subcontractor) report for a date into one PDF — subs submit their own dailies free and they roll up ([Daily Reports in Raken](https://help.rakenapp.com/en/articles/14465435-daily-reports-in-raken)). Compliance tab bulk-downloads a date range as a ZIP of individual PDFs; Project Summary CSV can be *scheduled*. 2026 AI: photo auto-tagging, AI daily-report executive summaries, facial-recognition clock-in ([rakenapp.com/features/ai](https://www.rakenapp.com/features/ai)).

**Praised.** Fast, mobile-first capture; "one easy-to-use hub" for dailies, photos, punch, RFIs; hands-on onboarding and support ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/raken/reviews), [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/construction/raken-profile/)).
**Top complaints.** (1) Pricing is a sales call and Capterra reviewers repeatedly flag **year-two price hikes**; (2) **Android app ≈4.3★ over ~2,400 reviews, a full star below iOS** — crashes after updates, checklist field bugs; (3) shallow integrations / customisation, no client portal or scheduling ([Contractor ToolStack](https://contractortoolstack.com/software/raken/), [Jibble review](https://www.jibble.io/construction-software-reviews/raken)).

**Reporting.** Branded PDF with logo, brand colours and weather on every daily; automated email distribution; Daily, Super Daily, Manpower, Project Summary, Tasks reports ([help.rakenapp.com](https://help.rakenapp.com/en/articles/14465435-daily-reports-in-raken)).

**Mobile.** Native iOS/Android with offline mode on the base plan; voice-to-text in the report flow; web is the office view (mobile has fewer features).

**Pricing.** Request-a-quote; aggregator ladder ≈ **$15 / $37 / $46 per user/month annual** (Basic / Professional / Performance), 3-user minimum, unlimited projects, **free collaborators** ([ToolStack](https://contractortoolstack.com/software/raken/), [Connecteam](https://connecteam.com/reviews/raken/)).

**External-party adoption.** Subs are "collaborators": no licence, they submit their own dailies which roll into the GC's Super Daily; the daily PDF is emailed to non-users.

## 5. CompanyCam

**JTBD / daily user.** Field techs and crews (roofing, restoration, trades) need every job photo out of personal camera rolls and into the right job, GPS- and time-stamped, so the office and customer can see progress and disputes are won ([features](https://companycam.com/features/)).

**Daily loop.** Open camera → shoot → **photo is filed to the project automatically by GPS**; no project selection, no form ([blog](https://companycam.com/resources/blog/companycam-features-you-need-to-succeed)). Project Feed is a chronological wall of photos + comments; @mentions fire an **immediate** notification; checklist assignments notify via bell + push/email per user setting; the in-app bell cannot be disabled ([How to Use Notifications](https://help.companycam.com/en/articles/6828347-how-to-use-notifications)). *Project Timeline* is a **live link** where customers see each photo as it is taken; *Galleries* are curated shares; *Showcases* are branded marketing galleries ([galleries & timelines](https://companycam.com/features/), [customer-update blog](https://companycam.com/resources/blog/project-timelines-photo-galleries-share-project-updates-with-customers)).

**Praised.** Capterra 4.7; "solves one problem completely"; auto-organisation, before/after, easy sharing with adjusters/customers ([SelectHub](https://www.selecthub.com/p/contractor-software/companycam/), [FieldCamp review](https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/companycam/)).
**Top complaints.** (1) Price growth and 3-user minimum phantom seats (Core now 1 seat; Crew/Scale still 3); (2) 2026 "bloat" — paying for AI estimating, payments, marketing you don't use; (3) occasional crashes, slow photo loading, "just a photo tool" ([ScanManifold pricing analysis](https://www.scanmanifold.com/blog-posts/companycam-bloat-2026-what-you-are-paying-for), [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/operations-management-software/a/companycam/)).

**Reporting.** *Reports* = photos + comments/descriptions in a branded PDF; *Daily Logs* and *Checklists* on Crew+; Crew adds "auto-generate job reports" and photo-verified checklists (required photo per item) ([pricing compare table](https://companycam.com/pricing)).

**Mobile.** Native iOS/Android with parity; "work offline, sync automatically" on every tier; annotations/markup, video, and 3D/LiDAR scans on higher tiers.

**Pricing.** Core **$63/month (1 user incl.)**, Crew **$129/month (3 users)**, Scale **$199/month (3 users)**, +$29 per extra user, annual; AI features Crew+ ([companycam.com/pricing](https://companycam.com/pricing)). Legacy per-user Photo plans quoted at $16–24 by aggregators.

**External-party adoption.** **Guest Access**: a project link by email/SMS; the guest verifies their email and can add photos and notes with no account or app, but cannot see replies ([Guest Access help](https://help.companycam.com/en/articles/6828446-using-companycam-with-guest-access)). Customers get Timeline/Gallery links, no login.

## 6. Novade

**JTBD / daily user.** Enterprise field-ops for large building/infrastructure jobs (strong in Singapore/APAC/France): Quality (inspections, checklists, handover, defect rectification), Safety (permits to work, observations, incidents), Reports (site diaries, daily progress with workforce hours), Workforce (check-in/out) ([construction-management-software](https://www.novade.net/en/construction-management-software/)).

**Daily loop.** Site diary/daily progress report per site; workforce **check-in/out by QR code or facial-recognition camera**; **scan a QR on an asset to select it inside a form**; push notifications for alerts and reminders; conditional workflows and form automation on Premium ([Novade Workforce](https://www.novade.net/us/novade-workforce/), [site diary app](https://www.novade.net/en/site-diary-app/), [pricing](https://www.novade.net/en/pricing/)).

**Praised.** Adaptable to project needs, secure/robust, timesheets and progress tracking, good support ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/novade-solutions-novade/reviews)).
**Top complaints.** (1) Pricey at enterprise scale; (2) app crashes with large files and photos that "won't save" (Lite); (3) some report sluggish customer service ([SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/construction/novade-lite), [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/novade-solutions-novade/reviews)).

**Reporting.** Auto-generated client reports in PDF or Excel from diaries and forms; custom dashboards on Premium; "Ask AI with Insights" ([Novade Reports](https://www.novade.net/novade-reports-en/)).

**Mobile.** Native (Novade Enterprise on iOS/Android), offline mode with auto-sync, geolocation on Standard+.

**Pricing.** Free **up to 5 users and 5 projects** (custom forms, basic PDF reports, tasks, storage); Standard **from $39/user/month** (geolocation, custom roles, notifications, credentials); Premium and Enterprise on quote; **no minimum users; View-Only users free on all plans** (view + comment, no seat) ([novade.net/en/pricing](https://www.novade.net/en/pricing/)).

**External-party adoption.** Free View-Only role for clients/subs; QR-driven, account-less interactions (worker check-in, asset scan); all collaborators on a project must be on the same plan.

## Synthesis

### What makes these the best

1. **One object, one screen.** Each winner has a single primary noun the field user touches: a pin on a plan (Fieldwire, PlanRadar, Dalux), a photo (CompanyCam), a daily report (Raken). Everything else hangs off it. E-Site's equivalent is the *board/node*.
2. **Capture removes decisions.** CompanyCam's GPS filing, Raken's auto-weather and voice-to-text, Dalux's QR-to-location: the app decides *where* the data goes so the user only decides *what*.
3. **Time-boxed pull, not constant push.** Fieldwire's 5-minute batching capped at 1/hour, PlanRadar's user-timed daily digest, Dalux's daily summary, Raken's self-scheduled reminder. Immediate alerts are reserved for direct address (@mention, assignment, due date).
4. **The output is a branded PDF the buyer never has to touch.** All six produce a logo-bearing, filterable PDF that can be emailed to non-users; Fieldwire and Raken can *schedule* it.
5. **Free for the people who didn't buy it.** PlanRadar (unlimited subs/watchers), Raken (free collaborators), Dalux (free unlimited users), Novade (free View-Only), CompanyCam (guest links). Only the core team pays.
6. **Native mobile with explicit offline enablement** — every product is native, and the good ones let the user pick which projects are cached (Fieldwire, PlanRadar) instead of guessing.
7. **Role narrows the UI.** Sub = update-only (PlanRadar), team-scoped task visibility (Dalux), guest = add-only (CompanyCam). Fewer options on site, not more.

### What they get wrong (opportunities)

- **Pricing opacity and renewal shocks** (Raken sales-only pricing, year-two hikes; PlanRadar cancellation trap; CompanyCam phantom seats). Transparent ZAR pricing with no minimum seats is a differentiator.
- **Offline is still lossy** (Dalux "reports lost out of range", Novade photos not saving, Raken Android crashes). Orphan-safe uploads with visible sync state are table stakes nobody fully meets.
- **Bloat drift** — CompanyCam and Novade are adding AI/FSM modules users pay for and ignore; Fieldwire gates basic reports behind Pro.
- **Generic to any trade.** None knows what a DB, a CoC or a SANS test is. Electrical-specific structure (boards, circuits, cable schedules, test readings) is E-Site's moat.
- **Guest participation is write-only** (CompanyCam guests cannot see replies) — an email-reply loop for subs is largely absent.

### Concrete lessons for E-Site

1. **[Fieldwire]** Batch task-change emails: 5-minute coalescing per record, max one email/hour/record, exclude the author.
2. **[PlanRadar]** Per-user digest with a chosen delivery time and a scope selector (all events / assigned to me / my team / none); default new users to "assigned to me, daily".
3. **[Fieldwire/Raken]** Scheduled recurring reports (daily/weekly/monthly) that auto-email the branded PDF to a recipient list — snag, inspection and forms already have the renderers.
4. **[PlanRadar/Dalux]** Free *sub* (update-only) and *watcher* (read-only) roles with no seat charge; only ORG_WRITE_ROLES are billable.
5. **[CompanyCam]** Guest link by email/SMS: verify email once, add photos + notes to a snag/visit without an account; extend it so the guest also *sees* replies.
6. **[Dalux]** Print a QR code per board/node; scanning opens that node's snags, forms and inspections — physical labels already exist on the Avery sheet path.
7. **[Dalux]** Public QR on the site board for anyone to file a safety observation without membership.
8. **[Raken]** End-of-day push reminder at a user-set time to complete the site diary, with auto-weather and one-tap "no work today".
9. **[Raken]** "Super daily": roll subcontractor diaries into the consultant's daily PDF automatically.
10. **[CompanyCam]** Auto-route photos to the project (and node) by GPS/geofence with a one-tap override; never ask "which project" at capture.
11. **[CompanyCam]** Live client Timeline link per project — read-only, no login, updates as photos land — as the default client-viewer experience.
12. **[Fieldwire]** Per-recipient filtered reports: one click produces a separate PDF per contractor containing only their open items.
13. **[Fieldwire/PlanRadar]** Explicit "make available offline" per project, with a visible sync-state chip, instead of silent caching.
14. **[Novade]** Free View-Only user type that can comment but not edit — zero-friction client onboarding.
15. **[All]** Publish ZAR list pricing with no minimum seats and honest renewal terms; every competitor's top complaint is billing surprise.
