# 04 — Trade-specific tools and the South African market

Research date: 2026-09-09. Scope: (A) electrical field-service and certification tools, (B) SA construction software and site practice, (C) tenant-coordination tooling. Every claim carries its source URL; where sources conflict it is noted.

---

## A. Electrical / trade field-service and certification tools

### Simpro (AU, est. 2002, 250k+ trade users)
- **Daily user / loop:** office scheduler + field tech. Tech opens the job on Simpro Mobile (Quote, Sales, Timesheet, Service, Stock modules), clocks on, records asset test readings, adds materials, gets a signature, syncs. [workyard](https://www.workyard.com/compare/simpro-review)
- **Electrical-specific:** an **asset register with per-asset test readings** (electrical readings, pressure, temperature) recorded on the job card or in the app; the app lists assets with **outstanding mandatory readings highlighted in red** until cleared; barcode/QR scan to find the asset; Maintenance Planner add-on sets test frequency per service level and alerts on failed tests. [simprogroup asset maintenance](https://www.simprogroup.com/solutions/asset-maintenance), [help: test assets](https://helpguide.simprogroup.com/Content/simPRO-App/Assets/Test-Assets.htm)
- **Praised:** job costing, workflow automation, open API (Xero, Ground Plan). **Complaints:** buggy mobile app, unintuitive UI, expensive post-rollout training, can't restrict data export. 82% satisfaction across 644 reviews. [selecthub](https://www.selecthub.com/p/field-service-software/simpro/), [joinsecret](https://www.joinsecret.com/simpro/reviews)
- **Pricing:** undisclosed; estimates ~$149/user/mo, ~$798/mo for 10 users, plus $2k–10k implementation. [workyard](https://www.workyard.com/compare/simpro-review)

### ServiceTitan (US enterprise)
- **Loop:** dispatcher's colour-coded board with live GPS and drag-and-drop; tech profile carries **licence type and certifications**, so dispatch shows who is *qualified* not just who is free; tech uses Pricebook Pro (Good/Better/Best auto-proposals), orders parts from the mobile app, takes payment on site. [servicetitan dispatch](https://www.servicetitan.com/features/dispatch-software), [pricebook](https://www.servicetitan.com/features/pro/pricebook)
- **Pricing/complaints:** $245–500 per technician per month, $5k–50k implementation, 2–12 month onboarding, 12-month minimum with penalties; BBB complaint "we have NEVER BEEN ONBOARDED … paid for 1 year". Built for enterprise; small teams struggle. [getonecrew](https://www.getonecrew.com/post/servicetitan-reviews), [projul](https://projul.com/blog/servicetitan-pricing-analysis-2026/)

### Tradify (NZ, est. 2013)
- Quote → schedule → timesheet → invoice spine, Xero sync "best in class", full Android parity. £34–44/user/mo UK, NZ$48–62. Best for 1–10 person teams. [softwaresorted](https://softwaresorted.co.uk/electricians/tradify-review)
- **Two catches:** **no offline mode** ("needs signal"), and native BS 7671 certificates are either absent or gated to the Pro tier (sources conflict — SoftwareSorted says not included; Checker says Pro-tier). Custom forms can capture test values against the job. [checker comparison](https://checker.app/best-all-in-one-app-electricians-certificates-invoices-uk-2026/)

### ServiceM8 (AU, iOS-first)
- Free / $29 / $79 / $149 / $349 plans; **electronic forms gated to $79+**; 250+ pre-made forms in the Form Store incl. an electrical inspection & test form and a 5-appliance PAT report. Forms can be made **mandatory checklist items at job start or end** — the mechanism that makes techs fill them in. [servicem8 forms](https://www.servicem8.com/us/features-forms), [pricing](https://www.servicem8.com/us/pricing)
- Praised: unlimited staff logins, geo-tagged photos on the job record, invoice-on-completion, compliance forms (CER, CCEW, AS/NZS 5033/5139). Complaints: Android lags iOS, add-ons inflate cost, weak reporting, no project management for multi-month jobs, ceiling ~50 field staff. [tradieautomate](https://tradieautomate.com/blog/servicem8-review-2026/)

### Buildxact (AU builders; estimating + purchasing)
- Entry $169/mo (1 user), Pro $279 (2), Teams $439 (4); 92% satisfaction. Complaints: pricey for small firms, basic reporting. [softwareconnect](https://softwareconnect.com/reviews/buildxact/), [selecthub](https://www.selecthub.com/p/takeoff-software/buildxact/)
- **Materials from the field:** Supplier Integration pulls live **supplier price catalogues** into the estimate, "recipes" bundle SKUs (door + jambs + hinges + lockset), and the PO carries the supplier's product codes so it can be fulfilled without a phone call; integrations with Bunnings, Beaumont Tiles; suppliers publish price files via an API. [buildxact supplier news](https://www.buildxact.com/au/news/product-news-supplier-integration/), [developer price file](https://developer.buildxact.com/suppliers-price-file)

## B. South Africa — software and practice

### Estimating / ERP incumbents
- **RIB Candy (CCS):** the SA contractor's estimating/BOQ/valuation standard; ~R1,500/month per licence plus one-time registration; planning module secondary; criticised for no AI in take-off and high licence cost. [leroux](https://lerouxconsulting.co.za/is-ccs-candy-construction-software-right-for-you), [capterra ZA](https://www.capterra.co.za/software/126567/candy)
- **RIB BuildSmart:** integrated costing + accounting + procurement + payroll; 4.3/5 (34 reviews), "a must have in the South African construction industry"; complaints: dated, slow support, heavy implementation. [capterra ZA](https://www.capterra.co.za/software/156457/buildsmart), [trustradius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/itwo-buildsmart/reviews)
- Neither touches the field: no snags, diaries, RFIs, certificates or DB data. They are office tools.

### Procore / Fieldwire adoption
- SA is "slow with the adoption of digital initiatives". **Bartlett Construction** (est. 1964; clients Growthpoint, Virgin Active, Lanseria) rolled out Procore Oct 2020 as an early adopter. Motive: the MD wanted "site instructions, RFIs, site diaries" without visiting. Most-used feature became **drawing revision control** — foremen measure to scale from a phone; outcome "consistency in the way information is presented from different sites". [construction world](https://www.crown.co.za/construction-world/projects-and-contracts/16931-bartlett-construction-building-smarter-with-procore-technologies)
- Procore = big GCs only; Fieldwire $39–89/user/mo (free ≤3 projects), offline-first, 87% of users cite offline reliability, per-seat cost balloons once subs and clients need access. [buildbite](https://buildbite.com/insights/fieldwire-pricing-review), [softwareconnect](https://softwareconnect.com/reviews/fieldwire/)

### JBCC / GCC / NEC tooling and the site diary as a contractual record
- **No dedicated JBCC/NEC administration software exists in SA.** The market is template packs (Setout: editable NEC3/JBCC/FIDIC admin templates with SA worked examples) and document portals (e-Cloud). [setout](https://setout.co.za/templates), [e-cloud](https://www.e-cloud.co.za/document-access-service/)
- **JBCC Advisory Note 17 (Ed 6.2):** a contract instruction must be **in writing** (cl. 17.5), "properly identified and dated with the receipt thereof signed by the contractor"; an entry in the **site instruction book or site meeting minutes is *not* good practice**; e-mail is "overwhelmingly the preferred medium"; the principal agent should agree the request/issue/record procedure at contract start. [JBCC AN17](https://www.jbcc.co.za/advisory_notes/JBCC_ADVISORY_NOTE_17.0Ed6.2.pdf)
- **JBCC PBA:** the contractor "shall maintain daily records of the number and categories of persons and plant employed on the works" and supply copies to the PA on request; the PA records and distributes meeting minutes. [PBA 6.2](https://www.nhls.ac.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Principal_building_Agreement_edition_6.2_June_2024.pdf)
- **Claims evidence:** "site diaries as signed off or checked by an independent party, or which have been submitted electronically" plus signed/electronic minutes are the core evidence for JBCC extension-of-time claims; clause 23 governs. [leroux EoT](https://lerouxconsulting.co.za/blog/successful-jbcc-extension-of-time-claims). GCC 2015 makes the Employer's Agent's Representative responsible to "oversee, record, report and certify" programme and safety compliance. [CESA PN47](https://www.cesa.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/PN-47-AND-21-Guidelines-for-Employers-Agent-on-Works-Contract-rev-2.pdf)
- **Snags:** under JBCC the PA issues the list for practical completion, followed by a 90-day defects liability period; disputes about the PA "adding items" are common enough to have their own explainer. [leroux snags](https://lerouxconsulting.co.za/blog/can-the-principal-agent-keep-adding-items-to-the-list-for-practical-completion)

### SANS 10142-1 CoC digital tools
- **ECA(SA) eCoC** (ecoc.ecasa.co.za) is the only SA-native digital CoC: web-based, "around 40% of the work is already done" via prefill from membership records; editable until **locked**; members R90 for 60 units vs R60 for 40 paper CoCs; top-up by EFT. Opened to **non-members Oct 2025 at R125 for 20 pages with unlimited Sub-DB and PV reports**. Ed 3 CoC is a two-page certificate plus test report; Ed 3.2 released 2024, Ed 3.3 in public enquiry 2026. [ecoc docs](https://ecoc.ecasa.co.za/docs/ch1/), [non-members](https://ecasa.co.za/member-support/the-electronic-certificate-of-compliance-ecoc-now-open-to-non-members/), [ed 3.2](https://ecasa.co.za/news/sans-10142-12024-edition-3-2-released/), [ed 3.3 draft](https://sapvia.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/SANS10142-1ED3.3_TC067SC06_DSS_1.pdf)
- **Fraud is the live problem:** CoCs bought with a contractor's DoL registration by a dismissed employee; admin staff holding the registered person's password *and* electronic signature; "never give anyone a signed blank CoC". The ECB accepts scans of paper CoCs for registration. [ecasa fraud](https://ecasa.co.za/member-support/dont-get-caught-up-in-certificate-of-compliance-fraud/), [ecb](https://ecb.org.za/coc/)
- Nothing SA-native does meter OCR, board scanning, offline capture, or links a CoC to the DB/cable data it certifies. ECA(SA)'s own "essential apps" list is calculators and vendor AR configurators (Veti, CBI Home, DesignSpark) — no workflow apps at all. [ecasa apps](https://ecasa.co.za/member-support/look-here-are-some-essential-apps-to-aid-south-african-electricians/)

### Site diary, snag and communication practice
- **Reality:** email for instructions, WhatsApp for everything else, Excel for snag lists and tenant trackers. The failure mode is universal: "WhatsApp groups multiply, photos disappear into camera rolls, and the moment a litigation, a snag, or a claim hits, no one can find the message that proves who said what". [sitesetu](https://sitesetu.app/blog/whatsapp-alternatives-construction-teams)
- **SA-relevant apps:** Site Diary (sitediary.com — offline, ZAR support, "records, records, records"); SnagR (UK; SA partner VDB Dynamics since 2015; capterra ZA listing); SnagID/SnagBricks (generic offline snag → PDF/Excel). **SiteSense** is Intelliwave's materials-tracking tool, not a diary. No SA-built snag or diary product with a review footprint was found. [sitediary](https://sitediary.com/), [snagr about](https://www.snagr.co.uk/en/company/about), [snagid](https://snagid.com/), [sitesense](https://www.intelliwavetechnologies.com/construction-software/)

## C. Tenant coordination / retail fit-out

- **TCTrac** (Rothschild Downes, US): cloud + mobile tracker of "all 12,000 points of data it takes to drive a tenant from lease to open for business" — DealTracker, ReportTracker, CostTracker; demo-priced; used by a consultancy that also writes the **tenant design criteria manual**, the document every coordinator works from. [rothschild downes](https://www.rothschilddownes.com/services/), [criteria manual](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/tenant-design-criteria-manual-key-retail-delivery-michael)
- **VTS Retail / MRI / Property-xRM** are lease-management suites with a tenant-coordination lens ("ensure tenants are tracking for timely move in"); **Fexa/Facilio** are multi-site CMMS for post-opening FM (Facilio can assign fit-out tasks per store and has a tenant app) — none carry electrical data. [vts](https://www.vts.com/retail-leasing-management-software), [fexa](https://fexa.io/retail/), [facilio](https://facilio.com/product/field-service-management-software/)
- **SA practice:** Growthpoint retail roles "manage tenant installations … monitoring their implementation and asserting budgetary control", run take-on/take-back inspections, induct contractors and keep "tenant and contractor databases" — i.e. spreadsheets. Large developments hire a PM firm for "programme and tenant coordination" (Mall of the North: Orion PM; electrical engineers Pienaar & Erwee). The electrical CoC "forms part of the paperwork behind an occupation certificate". No SA tenant-coordination software found. [growthpoint jobs](https://www.myjobmag.co.za/jobs-at/growthpoint-properties), [mall of the north](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mall_of_the_North), [shopfitting](https://durbanbuildingcontractors.co.za/commercial-building/shopfitting/)

## Synthesis

### What the trade-specific winners do that generic PM tools don't
1. **The compliance document is the unit of work.** iCertifi/Tradecert/Elec-Mate/eCoC all start from the certificate, not the task; the electrician's daily loop ends when the cert leaves site. Generic PM tools (Procore, Fieldwire) end at "task closed".
2. **They remove keystrokes at the DB.** Meter OCR, board-photo circuit extraction, voice results, prefilled contractor/registered-person data ("40% already done"). Every minute at the board is unpaid admin unless the tool absorbs it.
3. **Mandatory-before-close gates.** Simpro's red outstanding readings; ServiceM8's forms-as-mandatory-checklist-items. Compliance is enforced by workflow, not by reminders.
4. **Offline is table stakes.** Every tool that lacks it (Tradify, ElectraForm, NICEIC Online) is punished in reviews; Fieldwire's #1 cited reason to buy is offline reliability.
5. **Asset registers with test history per asset**, scan-to-find, and service-level frequencies (Simpro) — the DB is a maintained asset, not a drawing.
6. **Live supplier catalogues + product-coded POs** (Buildxact) turn a field materials list into a fulfilment-ready order without a phone call.
7. **Flat, unlimited pricing** wins over per-certificate (ElectraForm £1, NICEIC £1.55) and over per-tech enterprise pricing ($245–500) for anything under ~50 field staff.

### What is missing in the SA market (white space)
- **A SANS 10142-1 CoC that is linked to the installation data** (DB register, cable schedule, test report) rather than a standalone PDF form. eCoC is a form filler; nothing prefills from the DB.
- **Anti-fraud CoC controls**: per-signatory device-bound signature, registered-person 2FA, immutable audit of who locked what — the exact weakness ECA(SA) documents.
- **JBCC/GCC-compliant contract instructions and site diaries**: an instruction that is numbered, dated, receipt-signed, and can never be "just a minute entry" (AN17); a diary that is "submitted electronically" and independently checked (the EoT evidence standard). Nobody in SA sells this; Setout sells Word templates.
- **Tenant coordination that carries electrical data**: SA landlords track tenant installations in spreadsheets and hire PM firms; no product links tenant → shop DB → CoC → occupation certificate.
- **Materials-from-the-DB ordering with SA wholesaler catalogues** (Voltex, ARB, Waco/Major Tech) — Buildxact's model has no SA electrical equivalent.
- **Offline-first Android** for SA sites — most trade tools are iOS-first; SA site staff are Android-first.

### Concrete lessons for E-Site (12–15, one line each)
1. Make the site form/CoC **prefill from the DB register and cable schedule** (breaker ratings, cable size, fed-from, circuit list) — E-Site already owns the data eCoC users retype. [eCoC docs, Elec-Mate]
2. Add **board-photo circuit extraction** to the Forms module: photograph the DB, get an editable circuit list to confirm on site. [Elec-Mate, Tradecert]
3. Add **meter-display OCR** for IR/continuity/loop readings into test-result fields. [iCertifi]
4. Show a persistent **"outstanding mandatory readings" strip in red** per DB until every required test is captured; block submit on it. [Simpro]
5. Make key forms **mandatory checklist items at job start/end** (isolation record before work, test report before energise). [ServiceM8]
6. Ship **offline-first mobile capture with auto-sync**, Android at parity with iOS. [Fieldwire, Tradecert; Tradify/ServiceM8 complaints]
7. Turn the DB register into a **maintained asset**: per-DB test history, QR label scan-to-open, re-test frequency and alerts — a post-handover revenue line. [Simpro Maintenance Planner]
8. Implement **contract instructions as first-class records**: sequential number, date, issuer, recipient acknowledgement timestamp, never editable, e-mail delivered — and refuse to let a minute entry masquerade as one. [JBCC AN17]
9. Make the **site diary submit electronically with a daily labour/plant count** and optional PA countersign, so it meets the JBCC daily-records clause and the EoT evidence standard. [JBCC PBA, Le Roux]
10. Add **snag-list rounds tied to practical completion** with a locked "PC list" revision and a 90-day defects-liability clock. [JBCC cl. 27, Le Roux]
11. **Harden CoC signing**: registered person signs with their own authenticated device, no shared signature images, immutable lock + audit — market it against ECA(SA)'s fraud alerts. [ECA(SA) fraud alert]
12. Extend Equipment & Materials with **supplier catalogues and product-coded POs** to the marketplace suppliers, so a DB's required list becomes an order in one tap. [Buildxact]
13. In Tenant Schedule, add a **tenant-delivery tracker** (lease → drawings approved → beneficial occupation → CoC → occupation certificate) with per-tenant status and a landlord-facing view. [TCTrac, Growthpoint role descriptions]
14. Price **flat per organisation with unlimited forms/CoCs**, not per certificate or per technician. [ElectraForm/NICEIC churn, ServiceTitan complaints]
15. Sell the **"send it before you leave site"** loop — PDF + email to the project team from the phone — as the headline daily benefit. [Elec-Mate, Tradecert testimonial]
